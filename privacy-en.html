Privacy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In short: Minimoon collects no data. The app works without an account and without any server run by the developer. It does not sell, share or use any data for advertising or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The optional answers given at first launch: first name, age, estimated screen time, goals and the profile derived from them.
  • Your Screen Time selections. Apple represents them as opaque tokens: Minimoon never receives a readable list of your apps.
  • Your blocks (name, icon, length, protection mode) and your schedules (days and hours).
  • The history of your blocks: dates, lengths, blocks completed or ended early.
  • Your preferences: language, theme, colour, timer style, daily goal, early-end rule, reminders.
  • The status of your Minimoon Pro subscription (active or not), as reported by the App Store.

This information is only used to make the app work: apply the blocks you ask for, show the countdown and your progress, and start scheduled blocks. It is shared between the app, its widget and its extensions through an Apple app group, on the same device.

What Minimoon does not do

No advertising SDK, no analytics, no tracking, and none of your data is sent to a developer server. Minimoon does not read your actual screen time and cannot see the content of your apps.

Subscription

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Minimoon receives neither your payment details nor your identity: the app only asks the App Store whether the subscription is active.

Notifications, Lock Screen, Siri and Shortcuts

Reminders for scheduled blocks are local notifications created on your iPhone. The countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (Live Activities) is displayed by iOS from the running block’s information. When you use Siri or Shortcuts, the request is processed by Apple under its own policy; Minimoon only receives the block to start.

Screen Time permission

Individual Screen Time permission is only used for the blocks you start or schedule. You can revoke it at any time in Settings › Screen Time.

Deletion

In the app, Settings lets you erase your history or erase everything and start over (when no block is running). Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone.

Children

Minimoon asks for no email address and no date of birth. Any answers given by a minor stay on their device and are never sent to the developer.

Changes

This policy may change along with the app’s features; the date above is then updated.
