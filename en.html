Support

How do I start a block?

On Home, pick a block (“Less social”, “Just essentials”…) or set a length on the dial, allow Screen Time, select the apps to pause in Apple’s list, then tap “Take back my time”. Minimoon is used with a Minimoon Pro subscription (7-day free trial with the yearly plan).

How do I end a block early?

There is deliberately no instant stop. By default, request an early end, wait three minutes (you can cancel at any point), then hold the gauge for ten seconds. In Settings › Early end you can choose a simple ten-second hold, or no early end at all. Phone and emergency calls stay available under iOS rules.

How do I schedule a block?

On Home, tap Schedule next to My blocks, then choose a block, the days and the hours. It starts by itself, even with the app closed, with an optional reminder five minutes before. A schedule cannot be changed while its block is running.

An app is still available during a block

Check that it is part of the block’s selection (or, in Allow only mode, that it was not kept). Some system functions always stay available under iOS Screen Time rules.

I don’t see the countdown on the Lock Screen

Open Settings › Minimoon and make sure Live Activities are allowed. A block started by a schedule while the app is closed shows its countdown the next time you open the app.

Manage, cancel or restore Minimoon Pro

Manage or cancel the subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On a new iPhone, open Minimoon › Settings › Restore Purchases. Payment and refunds are handled by Apple.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone: your blocks, history and answers are never sent to the developer. Read the privacy policy.
